About

I build end-to-end data and machine learning systems that transform raw business data into production-ready intelligence.

My primary interests lie at the intersection of E-Commerce Data, Data Engineering, and Machine Learning — particularly in designing the journey from OLTP transactional systems to analytical OLAP environments, and ultimately into scalable machine learning pipelines and production applications.

I’m interested in the entire lifecycle of data and machine learning: from data ingestion, transformation, and warehousing, to feature engineering, model development, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and continuous training.

For me, a machine learning model is only one part of the solution. The real challenge — and what I enjoy most — is engineering the complete system around it, building pipelines that can continuously process data, improve models, and deliver intelligence reliably in production.
