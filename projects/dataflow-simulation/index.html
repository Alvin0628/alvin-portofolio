Overview

DataFlow Simulation reproduces how a data platform team operates a growing e-commerce business over time. Instead of loading a static dataset once, the pipeline replays a Looker-style dataset one month at a time.

usersordersorder_itemsproductsinventory_itemsdistribution_centersevents
Goal

Practice the real discipline

Incremental loading, layered transformations, orchestration, and MLOps, against data large and dynamic enough to make those problems real.

What happens on every batch

Step 01

Loads that month's data into the raw → operational schema.

Step 02

Rebuilds the Silver (cleaned) and Gold (business-ready) layers.

Step 03

Syncs the Gold marts to hosted Postgres (Supabase) that the dashboard reads.

Step 04

Runs inference for two ML models, and periodically retrains them.

Step 05

Refreshes a Next.js dashboard showing pipeline state, models, and business metrics.

Honest scope

This is a simulation: it replays historical CSV data in monthly increments instead of ingesting live traffic, so real-time claims do not apply.
