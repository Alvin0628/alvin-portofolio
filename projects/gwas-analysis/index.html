Overview

Which genetic variants separate Golden Retrievers that are vulnerable to cancer from those that are resistant? This project scans every SNP on chromosome 1 for association with cancer status, isolates the three strongest signals in DAPK1, TRPM3 and EYA4, and then tests how much those three loci alone can predict.

The approach

Scan wide, then model narrow

A TASSEL association scan ranks every SNP. The three top hits become a compact genotype table that is explored with cross-tabs and PCA, modeled with XGBoost, and explained with SHAP for the whole cohort and LIME for individual dogs.
