Overview

This project explores three decades of ASEAN macroeconomic history through the lens of SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth. Using IMF World Economic Outlook data across 9 ASEAN economies, we trace how each country navigated three major global crises, with a focus on Indonesia's macroeconomic trajectory and resilience — where it led the region, and where structural gaps remain.

The approach

Read growth through crises, not in isolation

Every chart shades the three crisis windows and benchmarks Indonesia against the ASEAN-9 average (excluding Myanmar and Timor-Leste for data sparsity), so a single growth number is always read next to what the region was doing at the same moment.
