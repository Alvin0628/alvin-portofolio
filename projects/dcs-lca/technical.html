The DCS-LCA mechanism

One expert, chosen per instance.

For every test instance, find its K-nearest-neighbor region, measure each specialist's local accuracy on its own class inside that region, and delegate the final decision to the best one.

Baseline

Probability Harvesting

Stitches one probability column from each specialist and renormalizes. Both methods share the same three trained specialists, so the comparison isolates the value of dynamic selection.

Proposed

DCS-LCA

Delegates each prediction to the locally most accurate specialist and inherits its full, self-consistent probability distribution.
