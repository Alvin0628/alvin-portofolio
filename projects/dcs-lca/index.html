Overview

Predicting student dropout on the UCI "Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success" dataset is hard for two compounding reasons: the three outcomes (Dropout, Enrolled, Graduate) are imbalanced, and they overlap heavily in feature space, especially around the ambiguous Enrolled class.

The approach

Rebalance the decision, not the data

An augmentation-free route: a pool of three class specialists (Gaussian Naive Bayes, XGBoost, SVM), each tuned for one class, is combined at inference time by DCS-LCA, which delegates every prediction to the specialist with the highest local class accuracy in that instance's neighborhood.
